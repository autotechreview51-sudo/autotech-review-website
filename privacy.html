Trust & transparency

Privacy, explained clearly.

AutoTech Review is designed to be useful without collecting unnecessary personal information.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Information entered on the website

The car finder, comparison tool and ownership calculator run in your browser. Your selections are not sent to AutoTech Review or stored on a server.

Partnership inquiries

The partnership form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not store the form contents. If you send that email, AutoTech Review receives the information through normal email delivery and uses it to assess and respond to the opportunity.

Email briefing

If you subscribe to the AutoTech buyer briefing, the email address you provide is processed through Netlify Forms and used by AutoTech Review to send requested updates. You can unsubscribe at any time. The address is not sold.

Automatic content

The website retrieves a small public data file generated from AutoTech Review’s public YouTube feed. Video thumbnails may be served by YouTube.

External links

Links to YouTube and other platforms follow those services’ own privacy policies. AutoTech Review does not control how third-party services process information.

Analytics and cookies

This version of the website does not install advertising trackers or create an AutoTech Review user account. Hosting providers may process basic technical logs for security and reliability.

Accuracy

Vehicle prices, specifications, incentives, financing and ownership estimates can change. Confirm important purchasing information with the manufacturer, dealer or relevant provider before making a decision.
