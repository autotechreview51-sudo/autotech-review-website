## Search submission

- Google Search Console URL-prefix property: `https://www.autotechreview.ca/`
- Submit `sitemap-index.xml` (includes general, video and image sitemaps).
- Inspect the homepage, `/comparisons/corolla-cross-vs-rav4/`, `/long-videos/`, `/watch/2026-corolla-cross-vs-rav4-canada/` and the latest watch page. Request indexing after ownership verification.
- Review Page indexing, Video indexing and Performance reports. Compare impressions, clicks and queries over successive 28-day periods. Search Console measures search performance without adding a visitor-tracking script to the website.
- IndexNow notifies participating engines of public canonical pages. The editor-publish workflow waits for the matching deployment. The hourly upload workflow notifies changed pages after a content commit. A 200 or 202 response is receipt, not proof of indexing.
- Feed readers can use `/feed.xml`, `/long-videos-feed.xml` and `/shorts-feed.xml`. Images and watch pages are generated automatically for future uploads too.

Google ownership verification and Google/Bing webmaster dashboards require the owner's account. Verification tokens must come from those accounts; no token is invented. Google’s general Indexing API is not used for ordinary video or article pages.

## Editorial links and return visits

- Ask an existing filming partner to link the relevant coverage from its own news or media page when it chooses to feature the video. Use the exact vehicle/review URL and accurate context. No partnership outreach has been sent.
- The live media kit, creator bio, editorial transparency and correction address provide a credible reference for brands and editors.
- Publish new coverage regularly; the existing hourly importer generates pages and refreshes discovery feeds automatically.
- Add answers to recurring viewer questions only after checking the video or primary source. Do not invent testimonials, firsthand verdicts, scores, chapters or tested performance.
- A newsletter can be added after a mailing provider and opt-in workflow are chosen. There is no nonfunctional signup or email collection on this site.

Search rankings, Google Discover and Google News inclusion are determined by their platforms; none is guaranteed by a sitemap or structured data.
